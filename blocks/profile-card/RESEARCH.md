# Profile Card – Recherche

Stand: 2026-09-26

## Aktuelle Leitplanken

- People summaries need unique, concise identification and only the metadata needed for the task.
- Actions must carry enough context to remain understandable for assistive technology users.
- Represent people consistently without forcing every person into identical amounts of content.
