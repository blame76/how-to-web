# Product Card – Recherche

Stand: 2026-09-26

## Aktuelle Leitplanken

- Product list items need enough information for fast comparison; title, price, image and relevant category attributes are critical.
- For visually evaluated categories, additional thumbnails can reduce unnecessary pogo-sticking.
- Variants should not create clutter that makes distinct products harder to scan.
